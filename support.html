AppMixer Support

Open a request on GitHub. We usually answer within a few days.

An app doesn’t appear in the mixer

Apps show up while they play sound. Start playback, then open the mixer.

Volume doesn’t change

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording and make sure AppMixer is allowed.

My keyboard volume keys stop at some level

A volume limit is set for that device. Open the mixer and click “Remove Limit”, or change it in Settings → Devices → Maximum volume.

A monitor (HDMI/DisplayPort) plays nothing

Some monitors stop accepting audio; AppMixer tries to wake them automatically. If it stays silent, turn the monitor off and on or choose it once in Sound settings.

Two devices are out of sync

AppMixer lines devices up automatically. Fine-tune in Settings → Devices → Extra delay for the device that sounds early.

Restore my Pro purchase

Settings → Pro → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
